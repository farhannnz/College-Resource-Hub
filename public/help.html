Getting Started

Welcome to College Resource Hub!

This platform helps you access study materials, stay updated with announcements, and collaborate with peers.

Use your college credentials to login:

  • Email: Your college email address
  • Password: Your assigned password
  • If you forgot your password, contact the admin

Access study materials easily:

  • Go to Resources page
  • Filter by category, subject, or department
  • Click on any resource to view or download
  • Admins can upload new resources

Stay updated with college news:

  • Visit Announcements page
  • Filter by category or department
  • Check priority badges (Urgent, High, Medium, Low)
  • Faculty and admins can create announcements

Discuss and collaborate:

  • Go to Forums page
  • Create new posts or reply to existing ones
  • Like posts and replies
  • Mark posts as resolved
